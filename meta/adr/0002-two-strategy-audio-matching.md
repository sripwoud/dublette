# Two-strategy audio matching

Audio joins image and video as a third media kind, but it cannot reuse the visual pipeline: videos are hashed _as images_ (one extracted frame), while audio has no visual projection. Audio files are compared per a per-run **match strategy**. **Recording match** (default) computes an acoustic fingerprint — ffmpeg (already required for videos) decodes ~120s of PCM piped to the pure-Rust `rusty-chromaprint` crate in-process — and groups files whose normalized fingerprint dissimilarity (0.0–1.0) is within a dedicated audio threshold (default 0.1); the existing hamming-bit threshold keeps its meaning for image/video, making **Threshold** a per-kind concept. **Encoding match** (strict) hashes the encoded audio stream with tag regions excluded — "same file, retagged" matches, a re-encode never does — and takes no threshold. Encoding match finds a strict subset of recording match's pairs; it exists for deletion safety (never group a lossless file with a lossy re-encode) and cost (no decode, I/O-bound). Because recording match deliberately groups files of unequal fidelity, its groups use a quality-aware keep policy (lossless > higher bitrate > alphabetical tiebreak, via metadata parsing); all other groups keep alphabetical-first.

Rejected: **fpcalc** (a second system dependency that links ffmpeg internally — two tools for one job); **fully in-process decode** via symphonia (codec gaps, a second decode stack diverging from video's); **decoded-PCM hash** for strict mode (full decode makes "strict" slower than fuzzy, inverting the cost motive — so FLAC↔WAV transcodes are intentionally recording matches, not encoding matches); **squashing fingerprints to a fixed-size hamming hash** to reuse `pairwise_compare` unchanged (loses alignment robustness to trimmed silence — accuracy sacrificed for code shape); **cross-kind matching** of video-embedded audio tracks (breaks the kind-purity invariant and forces the keep policy to rank a video against a FLAC — a music video and its rip are not duplicates). Missing ffmpeg warns and skips the audio pass (video precedent); silently falling back to encoding match was rejected because it changes what "duplicate" means mid-run. Revisit triggers: a real demand for video↔audio matching, or rusty-chromaprint fingerprint quality proving inadequate (→ fpcalc).
